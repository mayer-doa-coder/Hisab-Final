## Column meanings

MAE is in units per day (bold = lowest in the shape). MASE is MAE divided by the training window's one-step naive error; below 1 beats naive. *Folds short* is the share of folds where real demand over the 7 days exceeded the forecast total; *units short* is the shortfall as a share of real demand. *Folds over* and *units left* are the same for forecasting more than was sold. Bias is mean forecast minus actual per day (positive = over-forecast); it is supplementary and used for no ranking.
